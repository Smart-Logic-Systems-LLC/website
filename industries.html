Core Accelerator Solutions

  • Financial Technology

    Financial services teams need infrastructure that is both fast to stand up and defensible in an audit. Our accelerators ship with infrastructure-as-code from the start, giving compliance and security teams a documented, repeatable environment instead of a one-off configuration nobody can explain a year later.

  • Healthcare & Life Sciences

    Healthcare organizations run on strict compliance timelines and legacy content systems that are expensive to replatform. Our Wordpress Accelerator gives healthcare marketing and patient-portal teams a containerized, backup-ready CMS foundation, so replatforming projects that used to eat a quarter now start delivering content in the first sprint.

  • Real Estate & Management

    Whether for a single brokerage or a multi-region franchise, real estate microsites must launch faster than a listing goes stale. The Wordpress Accelerator rapidly spins up consistent, on-brand sites for new markets, while the Cloud Application Accelerator instantly deploys production-ready lead-capture and CRM integrations.

    Data center and colocation providers demand the highest infrastructure standards. That’s why our accelerators ship with infrastructure-as-code and documented environments from day one. Customer portals and internal tools inherit this audit-ready foundation, ensuring security reviews never become a bottleneck.
